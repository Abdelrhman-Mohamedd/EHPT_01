=== NexaCorp Information Security Guidelines ===

1. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days.
2. Remote access is strictly permitted through official VPN connections only.
3. System administrators must monitor service pool logs regularly.
4. Open ports and diagnostic endpoints are restricted to authorized subnets.
5. All employee-facing portals must undergo annual penetration testing.
6. File uploads are restricted to approved document types only.

For the full corporate security policy, please refer to the Corporate Use Policy document.
