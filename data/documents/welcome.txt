=== NexaCorp Employee Onboarding & Portal Guide ===

Welcome to NexaCorp!

This internal portal allows employees to access core administrative tools, request IT support, view corporate policies, and submit human resources documentation.

Key Guidelines:
1. Always keep your employee credentials secure.
2. Store documents according to corporate classification rules.
3. For network troubleshooting, use the IT Tools section.
4. Upload all annual performance evaluations via the HR Portal.

Contact: it-support@nexacorp.local
